# Stage 0 — How the dataset-level posterior responds to column mixtures

- **Date:** 2026-05-29
- **ADR:** docs/decisions/0006 (column-level missingness), Stage 0
- **Control arm:** `stage0_general_baseline` / RUN-071 — current-code general-tabular baseline
  (`calibrated.pt`, T=1.507, 10 missingness features / 77-dim gate, ~79–82% acc, ECE 0.056).
  This is the current-code analogue of the dissertation model (RUN-054 / Experiment 10,
  tagged `v1.0-canonical`), which current code cannot load (16-feature gate).
- **Status:** complete; motivates Stage 1.

## Question

The model emits ONE posterior P(MCAR/MAR/MNAR) per dataset and was trained only on
single-mechanism datasets. When a dataset is a *mixture* of column-level mechanisms, does
that posterior (a) track the composition, (b) collapse to the most-severe mechanism present,
(c) follow the majority by count, or (d) diffuse (entropy up)? No new training — the control
arm is frozen.

## Setup

Per held-out dataset (banknote, heart_disease, ionosphere, segmentation, glass, ecoli, iris),
we sweep the column composition between two mechanisms (and a 3-way equal point), reserving one
fully-observed clean MAR predictor, holding the per-column missing rate ≈ constant (~0.25–0.32)
to control the miss-rate confound. Mixtures are built with `lacuna.data.mixed_missingness`
(clean-MAR regime). 560 trials, 5 seeds/cell, deterministic seeds. Generated by
`scripts/stage0_mixture_response.py`; raw records in the run's `stage0_mixture_response.json`.

**Argmax flips** (count of the 35 trials per cell):

| Sweep | frac_b=0.00 | frac_b=0.25 |
|---|---|---|
| MAR→MNAR | MAR 23/35 | **MNAR 23/35** |
| MCAR→MAR | MCAR 25/35 | **MNAR 25/35** (no MNAR present!) |

- Pure MAR (MCAR→MAR, frac_b=1.0): MAR 17/35 vs MNAR 16/35 — barely a coin flip.

## Confound check (miss-rate held ~constant)

Mean per-column missing rate by frac_b: **MAR→MNAR 0.318 → 0.300** (flat, slightly falling)
while P(MNAR) climbs 0.379 → 0.652 — so the collapse is **compositional, not a
missingness-quantity artifact**. The two MCAR sweeps carry a mild ~6 pp rate rise
(0.25 → 0.31, since MAR/MNAR marginals run ~0.31 vs MCAR 0.25), but the effect (argmax flips
by 25%) is far too large to attribute to that, and the clean MAR→MNAR sweep has no such rise.

## Interpretation — "MNAR is a low-threshold attractor"

The dataset-level posterior does **not** track composition and does **not** majority-vote.

1. **MNAR contamination dominates at ~25%.** As little as 25% MNAR columns flips the label to
   MNAR (argmax MAR→MNAR in 23/35; P(MNAR) 0.38→0.57). The crossover is well below 50% — more
   aggressive than majority vote — and **25% MNAR is nearly indistinguishable from 100% MNAR**
   (P(MNAR) 0.565 vs 0.612). The posterior saturates almost immediately.
2. **Any non-MCAR structure routes to MNAR — even with zero MNAR present.** Adding 25% MAR
   columns to an MCAR dataset yields MNAR in 25/35 trials; the balanced 3-way mixture → MNAR
   74%, MAR 0%. The model reads "structured missingness" as MNAR.
3. **MAR is the systematic loser.** MAR is the argmax only at the pure-MAR endpoint, and even
   then barely (17/35). Every mixture containing MAR loses MAR, usually to MNAR — the
   dataset-level analogue of the dissertation's documented MAR-underdetection, amplified by
   mixing.
4. **Confidently wrong, not honestly uncertain.** Entropy stays moderate (0.55–0.69) and is
   *lowest* on MNAR-heavy mixtures. The model does not flag mixtures as ambiguous — it commits,
   usually to MNAR. The single-posterior output gives the user no "this is mixed" signal.
5. **Endpoints are sane.** Pure MCAR → MCAR (≈0.62), pure MAR → MAR (≈0.52), pure MNAR → MNAR
   (≈0.61). The model isn't broken; the collapse is specific to *mixtures*.

## Implications

- This is the empirical demonstration of the conflation in ADR-0006: the dataset-level
  posterior conflates "how much MNAR" with "is it MNAR", and on mixtures it collapses to a
  near-binary MNAR-or-not signal that **erases composition** — a 75%-MAR/25%-MNAR dataset and a
  100%-MNAR dataset look the same to it.
- The collapse is *directionally* conservative (toward MNAR, the safe imputation call) but
  **not principled**: it fires MNAR on MCAR+MAR mixtures with no MNAR content and destroys MAR
  entirely. It is uncalibrated conservatism, not risk-aware aggregation.
- Motivates **Stage 1** (per-column head): the column-level output is exactly what recovers the
  75%-MAR/25%-MNAR vs 100%-MNAR distinction this baseline flattens, and gives an explicit
  "mixed" signal instead of a silent MNAR collapse.

## Caveats / scope

- Mixtures use three canonical generators (MCARBernoulli, MARLogistic, MNARLogistic) targeted
  per-column — a deliberate, documented subset of the 110-generator registry (controlled,
  interpretable). Mechanisms are in-distribution per-column; only the cross-column *mixing* is
  novel.
- Control arm is the current-code baseline (10 features), not the 16-feature dissertation model
  (RUN-054). Whether RUN-054 collapses identically is untested (it isn't loadable by current
  code); the *qualitative* MNAR-attractor behaviour is expected to hold as it mirrors the
  documented MAR→MNAR leakage.
- "Clean-MAR regime" only; the entangled regime (MAR predictors themselves missing) is a later
  ablation per ADR-0006.
